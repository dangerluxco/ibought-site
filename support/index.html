Common questions

Do you see my Amazon password?

No. You sign in on Amazon’s own page inside the app. The password goes to Amazon only.

Where is my purchase list kept?

On your phone. If you choose to create an iBought account, a copy is also kept in that account so you can use the list at ibought.online/app. Without an account there is no server copy.

How do I use iBought in a browser?

In the app, open Settings and tap “Use iBought on the web” to create an account. Then sign in with the same email and password at ibought.online/app. Bringing in purchases from Amazon still happens in the iPhone app.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings, tap your account, then “Delete account”. On the website: Account, then “Delete account”. This removes the account and everything stored in it.

The import found nothing, or stopped early.

Amazon sometimes asks for an extra security check. Open Settings, tap “Get purchases from Amazon” and finish the check on Amazon’s page. You can also add any item by pasting its link.

How do I remove an item?

Open it and tap the bin icon. This changes nothing on Amazon.

How do I delete everything?

Settings, then “Delete everything”. Deleting the app does the same.
