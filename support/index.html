Common questions

Do you see my Amazon password?

No. You sign in on Amazon’s own page inside the app. The password goes to Amazon only.

Where is my purchase list kept?

Only on your phone. There is no iBought account and no server copy.

The import found nothing, or stopped early.

Amazon sometimes asks for an extra security check. Open Settings, tap “Get purchases from Amazon” and finish the check on Amazon’s page. You can also add any item by pasting its link.

How do I remove an item?

Open it and tap the bin icon. This changes nothing on Amazon.

How do I delete everything?

Settings, then “Delete everything”. Deleting the app does the same.
