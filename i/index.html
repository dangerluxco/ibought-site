A friend invited you to iBought

iBought is a free iPhone app that keeps a list of the things you have bought, so when someone asks “which one did you get?” you can send the exact item in two taps.

Get iBought free on the App Store

Your invite code

  1. Install iBought and open it.
  2. In Settings, tap “Use iBought on the web”, then “Create account”.
  3. Type this code in the “Invite code” box, so your friend knows you joined.

An account is optional. Without one the app works the same, and your list stays only on your phone.

Create an account in your browser and the code is filled in for you.
