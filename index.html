How it works

1

Bring in your purchases

Sign in on Amazon’s own page and iBought builds your list. You can also add an item by pasting its link.

2

Pick one

Search your list, open the item, and add a line on why you like it.

3

Send it

Text it, or share it through any app on your phone.

Private by design

It stays on your phone

Your purchase list is stored on your phone. An account is optional; without one, iBought keeps no copy on a server.

Your password stays with Amazon

You sign in on Amazon’s page. iBought never sees your password.

You choose what friends see

A friend sees an item only when you choose to share it.
