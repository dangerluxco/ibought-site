Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

iBought is an iPhone app that lets you keep a list of things you have bought and share them with friends. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Your purchase list stays on your phone. An account is optional: if you never create one, we store nothing about you. If you do create one, a copy of your list is kept in it so you can use iBought in a browser. We do not sell or share your information, and we do not track you.

What the app stores

  • Your purchase list: item names, pictures, product links and purchase dates that you bring in from Amazon, from an Amazon data file, or by pasting a link.
  • Your notes and share history: the lines you write about an item and a count of how often you shared it.

Without an account, all of this is stored only on your device. Deleting the app, or tapping “Delete everything” in Settings, removes it.

Optional account

You can create an iBought account with an email address and a password to see and share your list at ibought.online/app. If you do, we store:

  • Your email address, to sign you in and to send a password reset link if you ask for one.
  • A copy of your purchase list, notes and share history, so your phone and the website show the same thing.

This is stored with Google Firebase, our hosting provider, in the United States. Only you can read it; we use it only to run the service. Your Amazon email and password are never part of your iBought account.

To delete it, choose “Delete account” in the app (Settings, your account) or on the website (Account). That permanently removes the account and everything stored in it. Signing out keeps the list on your phone and stops the copying.

Signing in to Amazon

When you choose “Get my Amazon purchases”, the app opens Amazon’s own sign-in page. Your email and password go to Amazon, not to us; the app cannot read them. Once you are signed in, the app reads your order list from the pages Amazon shows you and saves it on your phone. You can end that Amazon session at any time with “Sign out of Amazon” in Settings.

What we collect

Without an account: nothing. With an account: only what is listed under “Optional account” above. The app and this site have no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Sharing

When you share an item, the app hands the text and product link to the app you pick (Messages, Mail and so on). What happens next is covered by that app’s own policy. Product links open Amazon, which has its own privacy policy.

Recommendation links

When you share an item, your friend gets a link to a page on ibought.online. The link itself carries the product’s Amazon ID, a short name and the code of its product photo. That part of the link is read by your friend’s browser and is not sent to or stored on our server. Your notes travel in the message you send, not in the link. The page’s “View on Amazon” button opens the product on Amazon.

Amazon links

iBought takes part in the Amazon Associates program. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases made through the “View on Amazon” buttons on ibought.online. This does not change the price you pay, and the person who shared the item receives nothing. Amazon, not iBought, handles the purchase and any tracking on its own site.

This website

This site has no analytics or advertising cookies. If you sign in at ibought.online/app, your browser stores a sign-in token so you stay signed in. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

iBought is not directed at children under 13.

Changes
