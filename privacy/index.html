Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

iBought is an iPhone app that lets you keep a list of things you have bought and share them with friends. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Your purchase list stays on your phone. We do not have accounts, we do not run a server that stores your purchases, and we do not sell or share your information.

What the app stores

  • Your purchase list: item names, pictures, product links and purchase dates that you bring in from Amazon, from an Amazon data file, or by pasting a link.
  • Your notes and share history: the lines you write about an item and a count of how often you shared it.

All of this is stored only on your device. Deleting the app, or tapping “Delete everything” in Settings, removes it.

Signing in to Amazon

When you choose “Get my Amazon purchases”, the app opens Amazon’s own sign-in page. Your email and password go to Amazon, not to us; the app cannot read them. Once you are signed in, the app reads your order list from the pages Amazon shows you and saves it on your phone. You can end that Amazon session at any time with “Sign out of Amazon” in Settings.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send your purchase list anywhere.

Sharing

When you share an item, the app hands the text and product link to the app you pick (Messages, Mail and so on). What happens next is covered by that app’s own policy. Product links open Amazon, which has its own privacy policy.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

iBought is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new date.
